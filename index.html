The Tally Cookbook

Take a picture of a recipe.
Get the numbers.

Home cooking is where every other calorie tracker gives up and hands you the work. Type in fourteen ingredients. Weigh each one. Divide by the servings. Do it all again next week when you use turkey instead of beef. Nobody keeps that up. So the food people eat most is the food they never log honestly.

Tally reads the page, or the link. Every ingredient gets its numbers. Every serving is counted. You are done before the oven is hot.

  • Made 15 servings instead of 18? Change it in a second. Every number updates.
  • Swapped ground beef for turkey? Two taps and it's done.
  • Cook it again next year and log it in one tap. You never build it twice.
  • From a cookbook, a screenshot, or a link. However you found it, Tally reads it.

If you cook, this is the reason to switch. We have not found anything close, and we would love to be proven wrong.

Everything you get with Tally

Every tool is one tap away. Use the ones that fit your day. Ignore the rest.

Tally Snap

One photo does it all. It reads a nutrition label and gets it right. No label? Tally Snap finds the numbers for you. Point it at a whole plate and it makes a good guess. Think the guess is off? Fix it in a few taps.

Smart Tally

Say what you ate in plain words. "A turkey sandwich and a handful of chips." Tally splits it into items you can change, each with an honest estimate.

Re-Tally

The fastest way to Tally. The protein bar and the smoothie you have every day are one tap away, for good. You never search for the same thing twice.

The Tally Ledger

Your whole day on one screen, meal by meal. What is left to hit your goal sits right at the top. See where your calories went, then plan dinner around it.

The Tally Cookbook

Take a picture of a page in a cookbook, or paste a link. Every ingredient gets its numbers. Every serving is counted. The food you cook at home finally has real numbers, and cooking it again is one tap.
